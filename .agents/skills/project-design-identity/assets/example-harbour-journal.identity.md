# Design identity: Example Harbour Journal

> A fictional example that shows a different direction from the other example in this folder. It is not a real project.

## Audience and context

Illustrative: regular readers of a long-form regional magazine, on laptops and tablets at home, scanning many articles per visit, in English. Sources: none (example). Unknown: share of readers who use dark mode, share on slow connections.

## Personality and feeling

Exciting and sophisticated. The target feeling is pleasant and activated: curious, a little bold.

## Signature decisions

- Colour: a vivid vermilion (hue 25) against cool neutrals, with an indigo accent (hue 265).
- Type: a geometric grotesk for headlines at large sizes, a readable serif for body text at a comfortable measure.
- Shape and layout: sharp corners, an editorial grid with varied rhythm, compact density on index pages.
- Imagery: large documentary photographs, credited and licensed.
- Voice: formal and precise.
- Motion: short transitions; one choreographed page-turn moment on the cover story, with a reduced-motion variant.

## Not like

Not like the health example in this folder (muted teal, soft serif, airy, illustrated), and not like generic magazine templates with a centred masthead and three-card feature rows.

## Studied references

ST-02: the exact text axis of a printed report, kept for articles and changed with vermilion blocks and a looser index. ST-03: a public-domain archive photograph for the cover story, cropped and darkened, credited in the footer ([design-study](../../design-study/SKILL.md)).

## Evidence and rationale

Read mode puts comprehension first, so body type and measure stay conventional [E-03, B] while the headline voice carries the novelty [E-04, B]. Contrast checked in both themes, both offered [E-17, B]. Photographs need licences ([13-visual-assets-and-licensing](../../../rules/13-visual-assets-and-licensing.md)). Vivid colour is a taste choice for this audience with no evidence behind it here: test.

## Open questions

Does the vivid palette tire readers on long articles? Compare with a muted variant in a reading session.
