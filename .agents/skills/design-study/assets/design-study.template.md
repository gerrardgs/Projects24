# Design study ledger: <project name>

> The record of what this project observed, imitated and modified. One row per reference. Delete this note.
> Check it with `node .agents/skills/design-study/assets/check-study.mjs docs/DESIGN-STUDY.md --brief docs/DESIGN-IDENTITY.md`.

How to fill a row:

- **Reference**: a link to the page or work, or `card:S-nn` for a study card of the skill.
- **Seen**: the date you looked, YYYY-MM-DD. Pages change; look again after a year.
- **Status**: `open` (openly licensed: CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, MIT, OFL), `own` (made by us), `licensed` (a paid or granted licence), `restricted` (all rights reserved) or `unknown` (not determined; treated as restricted).
- **Observed**: start with `Fact:` (measured or read: a size, a count, a behaviour) or `Impression:` (your reading). Keep the two apart.
- **Imitated**: the technique, structure or pattern you practised.
- **Modified**: what you changed so it fits this project and its identity brief. Required.
- **Scope**: `technique`, `structure` or `pattern` for anything. `asset`, `text` or `code` only for `open`, `own` or `licensed` references, with the licence and credit in **Credit**.
- **Used in**: the project (and screen) where the result lives.
